Cross-border telework and social security: the under-50% framework.

Belgium’s FPS Social Security (depositary of the multilateral framework agreement) explains the path: with employer and employee consent, workers may stay under the social-security legislation of the employer’s state when habitual cross-border telework in the residence state is less than 50% of total working time. Signatories include Luxembourg, France, Belgium, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands and Switzerland (among others).
