### Recommendation (labelled)

**Recommend: 20% of net subscription revenue for the first 12 months** after each referred **company** converts from trial to paid, paid monthly in arrears while the customer remains paying within that window; no share on individual plans unless Gus extends later.

- Matches Pilot’s **Provider** tier (warm intros / boutique consultancies — closest to fiduciaries).  
- Sits in the middle of Binhex referrer tiers and Lua’s sales-partner band.  
- Cap at 12 months keeps CAC predictable for a solo product; revisit “lifetime 15%” only if partners ask for longer economics.  
- Ententes shows the market also buys **white-label rebill** later; that is a separate product decision, not this batch’s offer.

**Alternatives for Gus to choose instead of the recommendation:**
