# Guides

Three guides in English explain how to count telework days for someone who lives in France and works in Geneva or Luxembourg, and how employers can keep a day-by-day record. They use the thresholds already on this site and the official sources listed on each guide.

## Geneva: 40% telework and 10 mission days | BorderDay

Live in France, work in Geneva? Telework from France stays taxable in Switzerland up to 40% of annual working time, including up to 10 mission days. Sources.

https://borderday.com/en/guides/france-geneva-telework-days/

## France-Luxembourg: the 34-day telework rule | BorderDay

Live in France, work in Luxembourg? Up to 34 days a year working outside Luxembourg stay taxed there. Social security uses 25% and 50% bands. Sources.

https://borderday.com/en/guides/france-luxembourg-telework-days/

## How to track cross-border telework days | BorderDay

Employers of cross-border staff: which telework records Swiss and Luxembourg rules call for, and how spreadsheets, HR systems and trackers compare.
